## Bug fingerprint

For each connection (initial + every reconnect attempt):

  1. TCP connect succeeds:
     [net.socket]   Connected to 66.241.124.119 (echo.websocket.org:443, mss=1168)

  2. TLS handshake succeeds:
     [transport.tls] Starting TLS 1.3 handshake with echo.websocket.org
     [transport.tls] TLS handshake complete: version=TLSv1.3, cipher=TLS_AES_128_GCM_SHA256

  3. extract_hot_state SNAPSHOTS BOTH SEQS AS ZERO:
     [transport.tls] TLS traffic keys extracted: cipher=TLS_AES_128_GCM_SHA256,
                     key_len=16, write_seq=0, read_seq=0
                     ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
                     this happens BEFORE do_ws_upgrade() — this is the bug

  4. Connection reported established:
     [transport.core] Connection established: echo.websocket.org:443

  5. (do_ws_upgrade runs internally — handshake_write(GET) + handshake_read(101)
       advance SSL's internal application traffic seq numbers, but the hot-path
       crypto context is frozen at the snapshot from step 3)

  6. First inbound application record arrives — hot path tries to decrypt with
     seq=0 but the record on the wire is at seq=1+ → AEAD tag mismatch:
     [transport.tls_dec] EVP_AEAD_CTX_open failed: record_len=24, seq=0

  7. RX worker triggers reconnect:
     [rx_worker.hpp:879] TLS decrypt failed -- triggering reconnect
     [STATE] DISCONNECTED — echo.websocket.org
     [STATE] RECONNECTING — 1/3

  8. Reconnect succeeds at TCP+TLS+WS layer, but the SAME bug repeats in
     transport.hpp:1244's reconnect path. Loop forever.

## Why "0 received" rather than "many corrupted"

Because EVP_AEAD_CTX_open is fail-closed: as soon as the AEAD tag verification
fails, the record is dropped and the connection torn down. The first record on
every connection always fails because it's the FIRST one with the wrong nonce
(seq=0 hot-path vs seq=1 actual). Server-side echo never even gets a chance to
respond because the client-side TX may also be encrypted with wrong nonce
(server fails to decrypt and closes connection from its end).
