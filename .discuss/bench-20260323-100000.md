## Summary
- ITCH parsing: ~0.8ns/msg (zero-copy, branch-only)
- FIX parsing: ~8ns/field (SOH-delimited scan)
- WS masking: ~14 GiB/s (vectorized XOR)
- TLS AEAD: ~5 GiB/s for large payloads, ~100ns fixed overhead for small
- No performance regressions detected vs. previous sessions
