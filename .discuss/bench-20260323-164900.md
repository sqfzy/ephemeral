## Assessment
No performance regressions detected. The format_double rounding fix and type safety improvements had no measurable impact on hot-path performance (as expected — changes are on cold paths or zero-cost at runtime).

## Key Performance Highlights
- **ITCH parsing at 600M+ msgs/s** — sub-2ns per message
- **SPSC queue at 1.6G ops/s** (zero-copy mode)
- **WebSocket encode at 19.4 GiB/s** for 1KB payloads
- **TLS AEAD at 3.8 GiB/s** for bulk data
- **FIX build+parse roundtrip at 203ns** per message
